About me

I'm a database administrator in Hyderabad and an Oracle Certified Professional (OCP) on Oracle Database 19c. For over four years I've looked after production Oracle systems that businesses can't afford to lose.

The OCP gave me the foundation. Production taught me the rest: building and running RAC clusters, keeping Data Guard standbys ready to take over, upgrading databases from 12c to 19c, tuning slow SQL and picking up the call when something breaks.

I also share what I've learned. I write on LinkedIn about real incidents and interview questions, and I wrote the Oracle DBA Interview Vault, a preparation book for DBAs with up to five years of experience, available on Topmate.
